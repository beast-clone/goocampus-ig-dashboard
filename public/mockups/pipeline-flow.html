4 · Overrun watch — “planned 45m, still going at 1h 10m”

Working-now = the plan block under the clock. If the clock passes a block's planned end and the task's status hasn't moved, both sides get nudged. This is how we know he's still working: time entered + no status change.

The one rule

A full day is never force-filled. Room is made only by a human: the producer accepting (his overtime, his call), or Manya moving a task (next day or a calendar date she picks). Every move is named.
