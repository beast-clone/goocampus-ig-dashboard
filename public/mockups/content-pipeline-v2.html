V1 vs V2 — the one difference
V1 — fixed hand-off

After the pillar, one button: “Turn into posts” → always the same 4 social drafts. Fast, but forces every pillar into the same shapes.

V2 — you choose (this page)

After the pillar, pick the format(s) you actually want — carousel, reel, blog, LinkedIn, poster — and it makes only those, each routed to the right place. More control, one extra click.
