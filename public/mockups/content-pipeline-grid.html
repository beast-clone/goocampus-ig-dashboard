Australian Medical Council (AMC) exam

The AMC exam is the main licensing pathway for Indian medical graduates who want to practise in Australia. In the standard pathway, an IMG completes credential verification, passes the AMC MCQ, passes the AMC Clinical exam, then supervised practice before full AHPRA registration.

What the AMC exam is

The Australian Medical Council assesses the standard of medical education and training. Its exams confirm that a doctor's knowledge, clinical reasoning, communication and practice are suitable for Australian settings…

The main AMC pathway in practice
  • Create an AMC candidate account
  • Primary source verification (ECFMG EPIC)
  • Meet the English requirement
  • Sit the AMC CAT MCQ
  • Pass the AMC Clinical exam
  • Apply to AHPRA · supervised practice · general registration
Fees & timeline

Guides cite total pathway costs around AUD 6,562, and roughly 2–4 years end to end…
