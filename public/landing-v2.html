Questions

The ones people ask.

Will it post something without asking me?

No. It watches, drafts and schedules, but publishing waits for a person to approve — from the dashboard or from Telegram.

Does it replace the sheets we already keep?

It reads from them and keeps them up to date, so nobody has to move. The master sheet in the Hub is the same work, editable in one grid.

What happens overnight?

Deliberately nothing. Between midnight and 6am every scheduled job is quiet, because no board publishes then and every check in that window costs money for no reason. Anything that lands overnight is waiting at 6am.

How does it avoid writing things that are not true?

Every draft is tied to the item it came from and checked against it before the copy is written. Where a fact is missing it is left blank for a person to fill, not guessed.

Why does it check messages before they are submitted?

Because a rejected message costs a day. The checker reads the draft, says how likely it is to be accepted and why, and shows a version that would pass next to the one you wrote.
