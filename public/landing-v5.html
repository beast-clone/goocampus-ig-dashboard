Questions

The ones
people ask.

Will it post something without asking me?

No. It watches, drafts and schedules, but publishing waits for a person to approve — from the dashboard or from Telegram.

Does it replace the sheets we already keep?

It reads from them and keeps them up to date, so nobody has to move. The master sheet in the Hub is the same work, just editable in one grid.

What happens when nobody is in the office?

The watching and the drafting carry on. You get the alert wherever you are, and can approve from your phone without opening the dashboard.

How does it avoid writing things that are not true?

Every draft is tied to the item it came from and checked against it before the copy is written. Where a fact is missing it is left as a blank for a person to fill, not guessed.

Why does it check messages before they are submitted?

Because a rejected message costs a day. The checker reads the draft, says how likely it is to be accepted and why, and shows a version that would pass next to the one you wrote.
