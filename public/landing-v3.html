12:00 AM — 6:00 AM

Nothing runs.

Not reduced. Nothing. No checks, no refreshes, no calls to anyone's API — because no board publishes at 3am and nobody is reading the screen, so every request in that window would be money spent on nobody.

It is the only part of the day that was designed by subtraction. Anything that lands overnight is sitting there, drafted, at six.

What that adds up to

Sixteen scheduled jobs. Two minutes of your attention.

The ratio is the product. Everything above runs whether the office is open or not, and the only step that waits for a person is the one that should: deciding whether something goes out with your name on it.

It runs without you

Weekdays, weekends, and the hours you are asleep — minus the six you asked it to stay quiet.

It tells you when it breaks

Every job reports its last run and what it costs. A silent failure is the thing this was built to end.

It never posts on its own

Drafting, scheduling and checking are automatic. Publishing waits for a person, every time.

Questions

The ones people ask.

Will it post something without asking me?

No. It watches, drafts and schedules. Publishing waits for a person — from the dashboard or from Telegram.

Why does nothing run overnight?

Because no board publishes at 3am and nobody is reading. Every check in that window is spend with no reader. Anything that lands overnight is drafted and waiting at six.

Does it replace the sheets we already keep?

It reads from them and keeps them current, so nobody has to move. The master sheet is the same work, editable in one grid.

How does it avoid writing things that are not true?

Every draft is tied to the item it came from and checked against it first. A missing fact is left blank for a person, not guessed.

What happens when something breaks?

It says so. Every job reports its last run, its errors and what it costs, and the morning check flags anything expiring before it stops working.

Tomorrow starts at six, with or without you.

Pick one board to watch. The next time it changes, the draft will be waiting before anyone has noticed.
