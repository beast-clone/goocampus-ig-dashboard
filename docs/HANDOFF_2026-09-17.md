### 2. Stories (new format, live in n8n)
Composer offers Post / Reel / Story; `enqueue` stores `publish_format: "story"` and
`type: "Story"`.

**The worker previously decided format with `publish_format !== 'post'`, so anything
not the literal word "post" was a reel — a story would have published to the feed as
a reel and stayed there.** Named formats now. Live in `frQoNFQjqVSnZsTp`.

- Instagram: `media_type=STORIES`, image or video, no caption, no collaborators.
- Facebook: `/video_stories` (3-phase) and `/photo_stories` (upload unpublished, then hand over the id).
- **Nothing has published a story yet.** First real one proves it.
