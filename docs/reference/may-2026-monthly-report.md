<aside>
💡

### 🔎 **Key Insight:**

- The continued focus on quality carousels, strong reels, and sharper hooks delivered outstanding results this month — content interactions, likes, comments, saves, and shares all hit new all-time highs, reflecting the strongest organic performance recorded to date.
- Engagement across all key metrics surged — Comments, Shares, and Saves all reached their highest levels ever, and Likes continued their strong upward trajectory, indicating the content is deeply resonating with both existing and new audiences.
- DM Leads: The continued rise in leads this month is driven by sustained promotional activity and organic interest across key markets, with the audience increasingly responding to the content and service offerings.

📈 **Growth Summary (April 2026 → May 2026)**

- **Followers:** Grew from 25,015 → 27,195 → **+8.71%**, the highest follower count recorded, crossing the 27K milestone for the first time.

- **Shares:** Surged from 7,948 → 10,590 → **+33.24%**, the highest share count recorded to date by a significant margin.
- **DMs:** Increased from 352 → 413 → **+17.33%**, a healthy recovery from April's dip, holding strong at historically high levels.
- **Total Leads:** Increased from 567 in April to 668 in May → **+17.81%**, driven by the Germany free live webinar and Australia AMC MCQ free introductory live class with Dr. Balan focused promotions, which continued to bring in a strong volume of enquiries this month.
