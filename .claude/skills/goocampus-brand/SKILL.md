# GooCampus — Brand & Art Direction (graphic / marketing)

> GooCampus = global education, career guidance & overseas medical admissions (study abroad, NEET/MBBS-abroad, AMC). Tone: **aspirational, trustworthy, warm, confident.** Not corporate-cold, not childish.

## ⚠️ Two brand systems — do not mix them
| System | Where it's used | Colors |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Marketing / graphic** (this guide) | Posters, social, print, banners, thumbnails — anything the public sees | **Orange + Navy** (from the logo) |
| **Product / dashboard** | Internal Marketing-OS dashboard UI only | Hope UI indigo `#3A57E8` |

Public-facing graphics use **orange + navy**. Never put the dashboard indigo on a poster.

## Color palette (sampled from the official logo — replace with exact spec if one exists)
| Role | Hex | Use |

| **Primary Orange** | `#F47920` | Energy, highlights, CTAs, the "goo" accent, cap motif |

| Ink / Charcoal | `#231F20` | Body text, the "campus" wordmark |
| Warm Off-white | `#FBF9F4` | Preferred poster canvas (warmer than pure white) |
| Muted Grey | `#6B7280` | Secondary/supporting text |

**Signature move:** orange + navy together (complementary), orange as the *accent* not the field. On navy backgrounds, set text/logo in white/orange (the logo's charcoal "campus" is invisible on navy — use a knockout/white lockup or set the wordmark yourself).

## Logo
- Asset: `public/goocampus-logo.png` (full lockup: cap "G" + "goocampus" wordmark).
- The cap = a graduation mortarboard drawn as a **G** — the core brand motif; reuse the geometric cap facets as a decorative element.
- Clear space ≈ the cap's height on all sides. Never recolor, stretch, add shadows, or place the full-color lockup on a busy/dark ground without a light chip behind it.

## Typography (approx — confirm official brand font)
- **Headlines:** bold geometric sans — Poppins / Montserrat (matches the rounded-geometric wordmark). Weight 600–700, tight leading, sentence case.
- **Body / subhead:** clean humanist sans (Inter / system) 400–500.
- Hierarchy: one dominant headline, generous negative space, minimal words. Let the message breathe.

## Layout & motifs
- **Anchor the logo** top-left or top-center; give it room.
- Use the **cap-facet geometry** (angled orange/navy planes) as background accents or a corner burst — echoes the mark without clutter.
- Big idea, few words. One headline + one CTA beats a wall of text (that's the poster rule; carousels can carry more).
- CTA = orange pill, white text, arrow. Keep contact/handle in a quiet navy footer band.

## Voice
Aspirational and direct. "Your global education journey starts here." Speak to the student/parent's ambition + the reassurance of expert guidance. No jargon, no hype-stacking.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Orange as accent, navy for trust, lots of white space, real logo, one clear message.
- ❌ Dashboard indigo, gradients-for-the-sake-of-it, stocky clip-art, 700-weight everywhere, black wordmark on dark grounds, cramped text.

## Formats (safe zones)
- Instagram post 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080 · Story/Reel cover 1080×1920 · WhatsApp banner 1080×1080 · keep text within ~8% edge margin.
